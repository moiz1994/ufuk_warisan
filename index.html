Our Capabilities

World-Class Infrastructure

❄️

Temperature-Controlled Cold Rooms

Built-in cold rooms with standing 3-door chillers (2 units) for temperature-sensitive items. Maintained at 1–4°C at all times with strict FIFO rotation to ensure zero spoilage and maximum freshness.

  • Dedicated chillers for berries, avocados & lemons
  • Cold rooms for large fresh produce & leafy vegetables
  • Ambient storage for dry condiments & tropical fruits
🚛

Comprehensive Transport Fleet

Our fleet ensures timely, safe delivery of all produce across Malaysia and for outstation customers, with comprehensive maintenance oversight for optimal conditions.

  • 🧊 Cold trucks — temperature controlled
  • 🛻 Pick-up trucks (canvased)
  • 🚐 Delivery vans
  • 🤝 Third-party logistics for outstation
